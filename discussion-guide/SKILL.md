# Discussion Guide — Study Companion Skill

Turns a completed video outline into a printable discussion guide for small groups, 
classrooms, or individual study. This is a companion resource, not a standalone document.

**Prerequisite:** teacher-foundation + a completed video-outline for this book.

---

## Input

Provide:
- **Book name**
- **Group context** (optional — "small group of 6-8 adults," "university Bible study," "self-study")
- **Length preference** (default: one page / 500 words max)

---

## Output Structure

### Header
- Book name + series title
- One-sentence summary of the book's theme
- "Based on [Channel Name]'s overview video"

---

### Before You Watch (2-3 questions)
Questions someone can engage with before seeing the video — based on what the book's theme touches in ordinary life. No Bible knowledge required to answer.

Example approach for Job: "Think of a time when something bad happened despite you doing everything right. What did you want from the people around you?"

---

### Key Terms (3-5 terms)
Plain-language definitions of words or concepts used in the video:
- Term
- One-sentence definition (no jargon)
- Why it matters for understanding this book

---

### Discussion Questions (5-7 questions)
Mix of:
- **Observation** (1-2): What does the text actually say?
- **Context** (1-2): What would the original audience have understood that we might miss?
- **Application** (2-3): What does this mean for how we think or live?

Rules for questions:
- No yes/no questions
- No questions with obvious "right" answers
- At least one question a non-religious person would find genuinely interesting
- At least one question that doesn't have a tidy answer

---

### Going Deeper (2-3 resources)
Specific, verifiable resources — not generic suggestions:
- One book (author, title, what it covers)
- One online resource (name the site or tool, not a URL — the teacher should verify these)
- One cross-reference passage (book, chapter — a connected text worth reading alongside)

---

## File Output

Save to: `guides/discussions/<bookname-lowercase>-discussion-guide.html`

---

### Closing Thought
One sentence that captures what this book uniquely contributes to the Bible's larger story.
Phrased as an observation, not a command.

---

## Format Rules

- No more than one page when printed
- Use plain language throughout — no assumed church vocabulary
- Bold only the question numbers and key terms
- Do not include answers to any question
- Flag any resource that needs verification before publishing: **[VERIFY]**
