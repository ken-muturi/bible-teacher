# Bible Teaching Channel — Curriculum Structure

A complete 66-book curriculum organized into 8 playlist series.
Each series = one YouTube playlist. Each book = one or more videos.

## Suggested Teaching Order

For a new channel, consider starting with high-impact entry points rather than Genesis:

1. **Start:** Mark (short, fast-paced, accessible)
2. **Then:** Genesis (foundational for everything)
3. **Then:** Romans (theological backbone of the NT)
4. **Then:** Psalms (emotionally resonant, widely known)
5. **Then:** continue canonical order within each series

---

## Video Format Recommendation

Each book = 1 video (15-25 min) covering:
- **What is this book?** (genre, author, date, audience)
- **What's the big story?** (narrative arc or argument)
- **Key passage(s)** (one or two anchor texts)
- **Historical context** (what did this mean to original readers)
- **So what?** (what does this change for us today)
