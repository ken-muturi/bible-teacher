What we assume

We think wisdom comes in large sizes.

The expert with the credentials.

The leader with the platform.

The one with the resources.

The one who is in charge.

So small feels like not yet — not ready, not qualified, not worth hearing.

Four things on earth are small

…yet they are extremely wise.

The ant

The hyrax
Little power

The locust
No king

The lizard
Easily caught

Where wisdom actually lives
Wisdom is how big you are
Wisdom is how well you know your size.

These four are wise because they accept their limits and live inside God's design — the ant stays an ant; the hyrax never becomes a lion. Their wisdom is humility, not ambition.
