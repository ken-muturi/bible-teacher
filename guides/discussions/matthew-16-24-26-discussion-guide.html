Commitment: Take Up Your Cross
Matthew 16:24–26
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.What three verbs does Jesus use to describe the commitment of his disciples?
2.What do you think the implication of "taking up his cross" means in the context of Roman culture? What event in Jesus' life helps to explain this?
3.What two types of people does Jesus contrast in verse 25? How are they different?
4.In verse 25, is Jesus only talking about physical death?
Application
1.What are some other phrases that would help explain the words "shall find his life," in terms of being Jesus' disciple?
2.What is the answer to the question Jesus asks in verse 26? How have you answered this question in your life?
3.How does Jesus say you can love your soul? What are the implications of this for a Christian and a non-Christian?
4.True or False: A Christian can expect a life of hardship and denial.
Prayer
Lord, we ask you to help us understand what it means to be your disciple.
Your words show us the commitment you ask of those who follow you.
Teach us to realise the importance of our soul.
You gave yourself for us; we will gladly give ourselves to you.
Quote
"Jesus has many lovers of his celestial kingdom, but few bearers of his cross."
— Thomas à Kempis, The Imitation of Christ
Memory Verse
Matthew 16:26 — "For what will it profit a man if he gains the whole world and forfeits his soul? Or what shall a man give in return for his soul?"
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your four memory verses from the last four weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
