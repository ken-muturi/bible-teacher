Seek First the Kingdom: Devotion, Treasure & Worry
Matthew 6
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.In 6:1–18 Jesus addresses three acts of devotion — giving, praying, and fasting — with the same pattern each time. What is the repeated warning about doing them "to be seen," and what is the repeated promise about the "Father who sees in secret"?
2.Look closely at the Lord's Prayer (6:9–13). Which petitions are about God (his name, kingdom, will) and which are about us (bread, forgiveness, temptation)? Which come first, and why might the order matter?
3.In 6:19–24 Jesus draws three sharp contrasts: two treasures, two kinds of eye, and two masters. What conclusion does he reach in verse 24 about God and money?
4.In 6:25–34, what reasons does Jesus give for not worrying (look at the birds and the lilies), and what does he tell us to seek "first" instead?
Application
1.Jesus assumes his followers will give, pray, and fast — but warns about the audience we perform for. In which of these are you most tempted to do a good thing so that people will notice?
2.The Lord's Prayer puts God's name, kingdom, and will before our own needs. If you compared that order to the way you actually pray this week, what would you notice?
3."Where your treasure is, there your heart will be also" (6:21). If someone looked only at your calendar and your spending, what would they conclude your treasure actually is?
4.True or False: In telling us not to worry, Jesus is forbidding all planning, saving, and provision for the future.
Prayer
Father in heaven, hallowed be your name; let your kingdom come in us.
Free us from performing for people — we live before your eyes alone.
Loosen our grip on treasure that rusts, and set our hearts on you.
Teach us to seek first your kingdom, and to trust you with tomorrow.
Quote
"He is no fool who gives what he cannot keep to gain what he cannot lose."
— Jim Elliot, journal (1949)
Memory Verse
Matthew 6:33 — "But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
