James: Faith That Works
The Book of James
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.In James 1:2–4 and 1:12, how does James tell us to regard trials, and what does he say the testing of our faith produces? What is surprising about "count it all joy"?
2.In James 1:22–25 James contrasts two kinds of people. What is the difference between a "hearer" and a "doer" of the word, and what everyday image does he use to picture the hearer who forgets?
3.In James 2:14–17, what does James say about a "faith" that has no works? What is his blunt conclusion in 2:17 — and how do you think it fits with Paul's "by grace… not by works" (Eph 2:8–9)?
4.In James 3:1–12, why does James give so much attention to the tongue — "so small" yet so powerful? What images does he use for it, and what inconsistency does he expose (3:9–10)?
Application
1.James insists real faith always shows up in action. Where is your faith mostly a matter of what you believe and say — and where does it actually show in what you do?
2."Be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves." Where have you been a diligent hearer (sermons, studies, this group) of something you have not yet started to do?
3.James says the tongue reveals the heart. Where does your speech — what you say, how you say it, what you say about people — most expose a gap between your professed faith and your real heart?
4.True or False: James and Paul contradict each other — Paul says we're saved by faith apart from works, and James says we're saved by works.
Prayer
Father, thank you that you save us by grace through faith alone.
But keep us from a faith that is only words — dead and fruitless.
Make us doers of your word, not hearers only who deceive ourselves.
Tame our tongues, sustain us in trials, and let our lives show the faith we profess.
Quote
"Faith is a living, busy, active, mighty thing… It is impossible for it not to be doing good works incessantly."
— Martin Luther, Preface to Romans
Memory Verse
James 1:22 — "But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
