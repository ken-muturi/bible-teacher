Sink the Roots: The Tree Planted by Water
Jeremiah 17:5–8 · Psalm 1
A rooted-faith series · session 2 of 2 (pairs with "Pull the Thorns")
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
"Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD… He is like a tree planted by water, that sends out its roots by the stream, and does not fear when heat comes… it is not anxious in the year of drought, for it does not cease to bear fruit." Jeremiah 17:7–8 (ESV)
Observation
1.Jeremiah 17:5–8 draws two portraits. What does each person trust in (v.5 vs v.7), and what is each one compared to (a shrub in the desert vs a tree by water)?
2.Both trees face the same heat and the same drought (v.8). What is the difference in outcome — and what accounts for it: their circumstances, or their roots?
3.Psalm 1:2–3 describes what the blessed person does ("delight… meditate day and night") and then what they are like (a tree by streams of water). How does the doing connect to the tree — where do the roots actually reach?
4.Look at Deuteronomy 8:3 and Joshua 1:8. What do these add about what the word is to us and how roots go deep (food… meditation day and night)?
Application
1."Where a life's roots go is where its trust really is." Honestly — where do your roots mostly reach for security: God's word, or your own resources, other people, or your circumstances? How could you tell?
2.Deep roots are exactly what you need because drought comes. Think of a past hard season — did your roots hold? What would sink them deeper before the next one arrives?
3.Meditation (Hebrew hagah) means chewing the word over slowly, not just skimming it. What is one practical, doable habit of feeding on Scripture you could begin this week to sink your roots?
4.True or False: A believer with deep roots will be spared the hard, dry seasons of life.
Prayer
Father, we don't want to be a shrub in the desert, trusting our own strength.
Plant us by your stream; drive our roots down into your word and into you.
Teach us to delight in it and meditate on it day and night.
So that when the heat comes, we do not wither, but keep bearing fruit.
Quote
"The reason we come away so cold from reading the word is because we do not warm ourselves at the fire of meditation."
— Thomas Watson, Puritan pastor
Memory Verse
Jeremiah 17:7–8 — "Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD… He is like a tree planted by water… it does not cease to bear fruit."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
