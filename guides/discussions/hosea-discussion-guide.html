Love That Will Not Let Go
The Book of Hosea
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.God tells Hosea to marry Gomer, a woman who will be unfaithful to him (chapters 1–3). Why do you think God asks the prophet to live out the message with his own marriage rather than only preach it? What does the marriage show that a sermon could not?
2.Hosea's three children are given the names Jezreel, Lo-Ruhamah ("not loved"), and Lo-Ammi ("not my people"). Later God reverses each name. What is God saying about Israel through these names, and what changes when they are turned around?
3.In 11:8 God cries out, "How can I give you up, Ephraim?" Look at the language around it. What does this moment reveal about what is going on inside God as he faces the choice to judge his people?
4.In 6:6 God says, "I desire mercy, not sacrifice." The people were still offering their sacrifices. What is God rejecting, and what is the Hebrew word hesed (steadfast, covenant love) pointing them toward instead?
Application
1.Hosea pictures a God who keeps pursuing people who keep leaving. When you sit with the idea that God pursues the persistently unfaithful, what does that stir up in you — comfort, discomfort, or both? Why?
2.What is the difference between religious performance — doing the right things, showing up — and the steadfast love (hesed) God says he actually wants? Where do you notice that gap in your own life?
3.Hosea repeatedly calls Israel to "return" to God. In everyday terms, what does returning to God actually look like for someone who has drifted? What makes it hard to begin?
4.True or False: If someone keeps failing God in the same way, there comes a point where God stops pursuing them. Talk through where you land and why.
Prayer
Father, your love reaches for us even when we turn away.
We admit that we wander, chasing things that cannot hold us.
Thank you that you will not let us go, and that you keep calling us home.
Turn our hearts back to you today, and teach us to return.
Quote
"To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is a lot like being loved by God."
— Timothy Keller, The Meaning of Marriage
Memory Verse
Hosea 6:6 — "For I desire mercy, not sacrifice, and acknowledgment of God rather than burnt offerings."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your four memory verses from the last four weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
