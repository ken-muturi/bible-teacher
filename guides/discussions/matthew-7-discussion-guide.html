Hearing and Doing: Two Ways, Two Trees, Two Foundations
Matthew 7
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.In 7:1–5 Jesus tells the person with a plank in their own eye not to fuss over the speck in their brother's. Is Jesus forbidding all judgment, or something more specific? What does he say must happen first (v.5)?
2.In 7:7–11 Jesus gives three commands — ask, seek, knock — and one argument about the Father. If even flawed human parents give good gifts to their children, what does Jesus conclude about our Father in heaven?
3.Jesus ends the Sermon with four sharp pairs: two gates/ways (13–14), two trees (15–20), two claims of "Lord, Lord" (21–23), and two builders (24–27). What single choice is being pressed through all four images?
4.In 7:21–23 some who prophesied and did miracles in Jesus' name are turned away. What does Jesus say actually distinguishes those who "enter the kingdom" — and what chilling words does he speak to the rest?
Application
1.Where are you quicker to spot the "speck" in someone else than the "plank" in yourself? What would it look like this week to deal with your own plank first?
2.The Golden Rule (7:12) is stated positively — not just "don't harm," but actively "do to others what you would want done to you." Name one concrete person and one concrete thing you could do for them this week on that basis.
3.The wise and foolish builders heard the same words; they differed only in doing them. Where are you currently a hearer — but not yet a doer — of something Jesus has clearly said?
4.True or False: Sincere religious words ("Lord, Lord") and impressive spiritual works are enough to guarantee that Jesus knows you.
Prayer
Lord, keep us from judging others while excusing ourselves.
Teach us to ask, seek, and knock, trusting our Father's good gifts.
Let us not merely admire your words but build our lives on them.
Make us doers, not hearers only — a house that stands when the storm comes.
Quote
"The Bible was not given to increase our knowledge but to change our lives."
— D. L. Moody
Memory Verse
Matthew 7:24 — "Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
