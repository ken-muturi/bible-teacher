Nehemiah: Rebuild the Wall, Renew the People
The Book of Nehemiah
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
Observation
1.In chapters 1–2, how does Nehemiah respond when he hears Jerusalem's walls are broken and its people in disgrace? Trace the steps from news → prayer → action. What does he do before he ever speaks to the king?
2.In chapters 4–6 the enemies (Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem) try many tactics to stop the work — mockery, threats, distraction, slander. How does Nehemiah respond each time? Look especially at 4:9 ("we prayed… and set a guard").
3.In chapter 8, once the wall is finished, the people gather at the Water Gate and ask Ezra to bring the Book of the Law. What happens as it is read and explained — and what are they told in 8:10 when they begin to weep?
4.The book ends (ch.13) with Nehemiah returning to find the reforms already unravelling. Why do you think it closes on this note rather than on the triumph of the wall's dedication in chapter 12?
Application
1.Nehemiah married prayer and action — "we prayed and set a guard." Which do you tend to do without the other: pray without acting, or act without praying? What would holding them together look like in a challenge you face now?
2.In chapter 3 each family rebuilt "the section in front of their own house." What is the "section of wall in front of your house" — the piece of God's work nearest to you — that you've been waiting for someone else to repair?
3.The deepest rebuilding in Nehemiah was a people re-formed around the Word (ch.8). Is your life being rebuilt around Scripture — or mostly around activity, effort, and getting things done?
4.True or False: A finished wall — a completed project, building, or ministry — means the work of renewal is now secure.
Prayer
God of heaven, you are the one who rebuilds what lies in ruins.
Teach us to pray and to work — depending on you, and taking up the task in front of us.
Re-form us around your Word, not just around our striving.
And let the joy of the LORD be our strength as we build.
Quote
"Prayer and pains, through faith in Christ Jesus, will do anything."
— John Eliot, Puritan missionary
Memory Verse
Nehemiah 8:10 — "Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your strength."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
