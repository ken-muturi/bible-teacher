Pull the Thorns: The Thorny Soil
Luke 8:4–15 (focus v.14)
A rooted-faith series · session 1 of 2 (pairs with "Sink the Roots")
I. Connecting
Share what happened in your lives during the last week. Use an ice-breaker or other approach to get to know each other better.
II. Seeking God
(If your group is larger than 8 persons, break into groups of 3–4 for this.) Discuss any personal roadblocks, challenges, or fears that threaten your ability to walk by faith. Pray about things that will really make a difference in your life today.
III. Lesson Time
"And as for what fell among the thorns, they are those who hear, but as they go on their way they are choked by the cares and riches and pleasures of life, and their fruit does not mature." Luke 8:14 (ESV)
Observation
1.In the parable the seed falls on four soils (8:5–8). In Jesus' explanation, what is the seed (8:11), and what are the four soils actually pictures of? What does that tell us the whole parable is really about?
2.Focus on the thorny soil (8:14). Name the three specific things Jesus says choke the word. And notice the timing — when does the choking happen ("as they go on their way")? What does that reveal about how it works?
3.What is the result for the thorny soil — "their fruit does not mature"? Is the plant dead, like the path, or is something else going wrong here? How is this failure different from the other two?
4.Now look at the good soil (8:15). What three marks does Jesus give it (an honest and good heart · hold it fast · bear fruit with patience)? How is each one the opposite of the thorny soil's problem?
Application
1.The three thorns — worries, riches, pleasures — are all ordinary parts of life, not obvious sins. Why does that make them more dangerous to your fruitfulness than open, scandalous temptation?
2.The choking happens gradually, "as they go on their way" — not in a crisis but over ordinary days. Where might God's word be quietly getting crowded out in your daily routine right now, without you deciding anything dramatic?
3.Of the three thorns — anxiety, the pursuit of money, or the pull of pleasures and comfort — which one is most actively choking the word in you? Name one concrete way you could "pull" it this week.
4.True or False: As long as I'm not committing any obvious sins, my faith is safe from ever becoming unfruitful.
Prayer
Father, thank you for the seed of your word planted in us.
Show us the thorns we have let grow up around it — our worries, our wanting, our comforts.
Give us the honesty to name them and the grace to pull them.
Make us good soil that holds your word fast and bears fruit with patience.
Quote
"The cares of this world are fitly compared to thorns, for they came in with sin, and are a fruit of the curse; they are good in their place, but hurtful when they intrude upon the heart."
— Matthew Henry, Commentary (on the Sower)
Memory Verse
Luke 8:15 — "As for that in the good soil, they are those who, hearing the word, hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit with patience."
IV. Accountability
Break into small groups of 3–4 persons. Recite your memory verses from the last few weeks. Share what you have done/learned in your daily devotion times. Share how you have sought opportunities to build relationships with unbelievers on your evangelism list, and the outcome of this.
V. Building Community
Do an exercise or have a discussion that builds stronger and deeper relationships. Make announcements and plans that affect the entire group.
VI. Closing
Close in prayer. Enjoy refreshments and fellowship.
