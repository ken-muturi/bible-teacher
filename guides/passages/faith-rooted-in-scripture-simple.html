Faith Rooted in Scripture
Rebuilding on the Word — a simple teaching
Amos 9:11–13 · Year theme: strengthening families & ministries
⊕ Go deeper — full deep study & commentaries →
The Text
"In that day I will raise up the fallen shelter of David. I will repair its broken walls, restore its ruins, and rebuild it as it used to be… New wine will drip from the mountains and flow from all the hills."
Amos 9:11, 13 (NIV)
The Big Idea (in plain words)
After a whole book of bad news, God suddenly promises something wonderful: he will rebuild what has fallen down. The fallen "shelter of David" — his people, his kingdom — will be raised up again.

And notice who does it: "I will raise up… I will rebuild." Not the people's hard work. God's own promise. The whole thing rests on five words he keeps repeating: "declares the LORD."

That is the heart of a faith rooted in Scripture: what will really last is not built on our effort or our circumstances, but on what God has said.
Three Simple Truths
1 · God rebuilds
He raises up what has fallen
A collapsed shelter cannot fix itself. God says, "I will raise it up." Whatever has fallen in your life — a family, a hope, a ministry — God is the God who rebuilds ruins.
Amos 9:11
2 · Built on his word
The rebuilt house is the church
In Acts 15, the apostles opened the Scriptures and saw that Amos 9 was coming true — the "tent of David" being rebuilt is the church, Jews and Gentiles together. God's people are known and founded by his word.
Acts 15:16–18
3 · Rooted & fruitful
Planted, never uprooted
God promises to "plant them… never again to be uprooted." The one rooted in God's word is like a tree by water — green and fruitful even in a drought. Deep roots, lasting fruit.
Amos 9:15 · Psalm 1:2–3
For You This Week
Name the ruin. What in your family or ministry feels fallen? Bring it honestly to God — he rebuilds real ruins, not pretend ones.
Stop striving alone. Where are you exhausting yourself rebuilding in your own strength? Rest on what God has promised in his word.
Sink the roots. Choose one way to root your home or ministry deeper in Scripture this year — a daily reading, a memory verse, family devotions.
Pull the thorns. Worry, money, and comfort choke the word (Luke 8:14). Name the one crowding it out most, and clear it.
Remember This
Memory Verse
"So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ."
Romans 10:17
In one line
God rebuilds fallen things on his own word — so root your faith, your family, and your ministry in Scripture, and you will stand in the drought and overflow in the harvest.
