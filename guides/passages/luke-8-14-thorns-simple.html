Pull the Thorns
The Thorny Soil — a simple teaching
Luke 8:14 · the Parable of the Sower
⊕ Go deeper — full deep study & commentaries →
The Text
"As for what fell among the thorns, they are those who hear, but as they go on their way they are choked by the cares and riches and pleasures of life, and their fruit does not mature."
Luke 8:14 (ESV)
The Big Idea (in plain words)
Jesus says the seed is God's word, and the soils are four kinds of hearts. The thorny soil is the scary one — because the word really was received and even started to grow. Nothing dramatic goes wrong.

But three other things were allowed to grow up alongside it: worries, money, and pleasures. Slowly, "as they go on their way," those thorns crowd it out and choke it — until the plant lives but never bears real fruit.

The three thorns aren't obvious sins; they're just ordinary life. That's exactly why they're so deadly. Weeds don't need to be planted — only left alone.
The Three Thorns to Pull
1 · Cares
The worries of life
Anxiety, pressure, the weight of responsibilities and money troubles that fill the mind so the word can't get your attention.
2 · Riches
The deceit of wealth
Money and the chasing of it, promising a security it can't give, quietly reordering your life until there's no room for the word.
3 · Pleasures
The pull of comfort
Good things loved too much — entertainments and appetites that take the time and heart-space the word needed.
For You This Week
Name your thorn. Which of the three — worry, money, or pleasure — is most choking the word in you right now? Be honest.
Pull it. Cast your cares on God in prayer (1 Pet 5:7); give money away (1 Tim 6:18); say no to a comfort that's crowding out prayer.
Guard the ground. Give God's word the prime time, not the leftover minutes. Weeds regrow — keep weeding.
Aim at fruit. The goal isn't a bare field but a fruitful one — the word given room to grow to maturity (8:15).
Remember This
Memory Verse
"…hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit with patience."
Luke 8:15
In one line
You lose your harvest not to scandal but to ordinary life crowding out the word — so name the thorns and pull them, and give the word room to bear fruit.
